MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement is made on 15 March 2025 (the "Effective
Date") between Halcyon Cloud Services Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Vendor"),
and Copperleaf Retail Group LLC, a Texas limited liability company
("Customer").

1. SUBSCRIPTION
Vendor grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access the
Halcyon Analytics platform for up to 250 named users during the Subscription
Term.

2. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
The initial Subscription Term is 36 months from the Effective Date.

3. FEES
The annual subscription fee is USD 180,000, invoiced annually in advance.
Customer shall pay each invoice within 45 days of receipt. Fees are exclusive
of taxes.

4. SERVICE LEVELS
Vendor shall make the platform available 99.9% of each calendar month,
excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance.
Service credits under Schedule B are Customer's sole remedy for a missed
service level.

5. CUSTOMER DATA
Customer owns all data it uploads. Vendor shall process Customer data only to
provide the services, and shall delete it within 60 days after the agreement
ends.

6. LIABILITY
Except for breaches of confidentiality, each party's aggregate liability
arising out of this agreement is capped at the fees paid by Customer in the
twelve months before the claim.

7. TERMINATION
Customer may terminate for convenience on 60 days written notice, subject to
payment of the fees for the remainder of the then-current year. Either party
may terminate if the other becomes insolvent.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE
This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without
regard to its conflict of laws rules. The parties submit to the state and
federal courts located in New York County.

Executed by the authorized representatives of Halcyon Cloud Services Inc. and
Copperleaf Retail Group LLC.
