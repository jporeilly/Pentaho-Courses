EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT (DRAFT FOR REVIEW)

Parties: Redfern Industrial Services Pty Ltd ("Contractor") and Marlow Valley
Water Authority ("Authority").

Background
The Authority operates four pumping stations and wishes the Contractor to
maintain their pumps, valves and control panels.

1. SCOPE
The Contractor shall carry out quarterly preventive maintenance at each
pumping station, and respond to breakdown calls within 4 hours, 24 hours a
day.

2. PRICE
The Authority shall pay a fixed quarterly fee of AUD 42,500, plus parts at
cost plus 10%. The Contractor shall submit a tax invoice at the end of each
quarter, payable within 14 days.

3. INSURANCE
The Contractor shall hold public liability insurance of at least AUD 20
million and workers compensation insurance as required by law.

4. LIABILITY
The Contractor's liability for loss or damage to the Authority's equipment is
limited to the cost of repair or replacement.

5. ENDING THIS AGREEMENT
Either party may end this agreement by giving the other 30 days notice in
writing.

[Commencement date and governing law to be confirmed by the Authority's legal
team before signature.]
