SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 1 January 2025
("Effective Date") by and between:

Acme Software Ltd, a company registered in England with offices at 14 Quay
Street, Bristol ("Service Provider")
and
Brightwater Holdings PLC, with offices at 200 Lombard Row, London ("Client").

1. SERVICES
The Service Provider shall provide software development and consultancy
services as described in Schedule 1. Each statement of work shall be agreed
in writing before work begins.

2. TERM
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for 24
months, after which it renews for successive 12-month terms unless either
party gives notice under clause 8.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
The Client shall pay the fees set out in each statement of work. The Service
Provider shall invoice monthly in arrears. Payment is due within 30 days of
the date of each invoice. Late payments bear interest at 4% above the base
rate.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All deliverables created under this Agreement vest in the Client on payment
in full. The Service Provider keeps its pre-existing tools and libraries and
grants the Client a perpetual license to use them within the deliverables.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret and use it
only to perform this Agreement, during the term and for five years after.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
The total liability of the Service Provider under or in connection with this
Agreement shall not exceed GBP 500,000 in any contract year. Neither party is
liable for loss of profit or indirect loss.

7. DATA PROTECTION
Each party shall comply with applicable data protection law. Where the
Service Provider processes personal data for the Client, it does so only on
the Client's documented instructions.

8. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving 90 days
written notice to the other. Either party may terminate immediately by notice
if the other commits a material breach that it fails to remedy within 30 days
of being asked to.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement and any dispute arising from it shall be governed by the laws
of England and Wales, and the courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction.

Signed for Acme Software Ltd: J. Carter, Director
Signed for Brightwater Holdings PLC: R. Osei, Chief Operating Officer
