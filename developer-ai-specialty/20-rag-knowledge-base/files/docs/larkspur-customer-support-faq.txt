Larkspur Customer Support - Frequently Asked Questions

Contact

Larkspur Service: 1-800-555-0142, Monday to Friday 8 am to 8 pm and Saturday 9 am to 1 pm. Online support and claims: support.larkspur.example.

Where is the serial number?

Dishwashers: on the label on the right-hand edge of the door. Washing machines: inside the door, on the rim of the porthole. Coffee machines: under the drip tray.

Spare parts and accessories

Genuine filters, water filter cartridges, door seals, hoses and DescalePro descaler can be ordered at support.larkspur.example/parts. Orders over $35 ship free. Larkspur keeps functional spare parts available for at least 7 years after a model is discontinued.

Installation

Larkspur can install a new dishwasher or washing machine on the day of delivery for a fee of $89, which includes removing the old appliance.
