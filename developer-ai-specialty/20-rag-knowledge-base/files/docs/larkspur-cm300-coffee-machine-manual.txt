Larkspur BrewMaster CM-300 Espresso Machine - Quick Manual

Getting started

The Larkspur BrewMaster CM-300 is a bean-to-cup espresso machine with a built-in conical grinder, a 1.8 liter water tank and a steam wand for milk.

Before the first use, rinse the water tank, fill it with fresh cold water and run two cups of hot water through the machine without coffee.

Indicator lights and codes

Red light flashing: the water tank is empty or not seated correctly. Fill the tank and push it down until it clicks into place.

Amber light flashing: the machine needs descaling. Run the descaling program (see Descaling).

Code C3 on the display: the grinder is blocked. Switch off the machine, empty the bean hopper and remove any stones or foreign objects. Use only roasted coffee beans; never put ground coffee, oily or caramelized beans in the hopper.

Code C5: the brewing unit is not in place. Open the side door, push the brewing unit in until it clicks and close the door.

Descaling

Descale the BrewMaster every 200 cups, or as soon as the amber light flashes, whichever comes first. In hard water areas this is about once a month.

Use only Larkspur DescalePro descaler. Never use vinegar: it damages the seals and the heating block.

To descale: empty the drip tray, remove the water filter, pour one bottle of DescalePro into the tank and fill it with water up to the MAX mark. Press and hold the Hot Water and Steam buttons together for 5 seconds to start the descaling program. It takes about 25 minutes and rinses the machine twice; refill the tank with fresh water when the machine asks you to.

Taste and troubleshooting

Coffee is too weak or watery: set the grinder finer, one step at a time, while the grinder is running, and use more coffee.

Coffee is too bitter: set the grinder coarser, or choose a lighter roast.

Coffee is not hot enough: preheat the cup with hot water, and descale the machine if the amber light is on.

No steam: the steam wand is blocked by dried milk. Unscrew the tip and soak it in warm water.

Daily care

Clean the steam wand after each use: wipe it with a damp cloth and let a little steam out to clear the inside.

Empty the drip tray and the coffee grounds container every day.

Replace the water filter cartridge every 2 months. A filter reduces limescale and extends the time between descaling.
