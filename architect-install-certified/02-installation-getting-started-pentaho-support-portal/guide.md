# Pentaho Support Portal

> **Note:** **Pentaho Customer Portal (EE)**
>
> Access Pentaho Enterprise downloads with your principal account credentials.

1. Log into Pentaho Customer Portal.

<div class="pcm-embed-card" data-href="https://support.pentaho.com/hc/en-us" data-title="support.pentaho.com"></div>

<figure><img src="../_assets/images/support_portal.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Pentaho Support Portal</p></figcaption></figure>

2. Click Pentaho > DOWNLOAD.
3. Sign In to Pentaho Customer Portal.

<figure><img src="../_assets/images/support_portal_login.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Pentaho Customer Support Portal - Log In</p></figcaption></figure>

4. Select your Pentaho version: Pentaho 11.0 GA Release

<figure><img src="../_assets/images/support_portal_version.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Select the required Pentaho packages to download.

<figure><img src="../_assets/images/support_portal_packages.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Packages</p></figcaption></figure>
